What Does a Confidence Interval Actually Capture?

The true population mean (red line) is fixed at μ = 2.25 — in practice we never know it. Each sample produces an estimate p̂ (blue line). Drag the slider to move p̂ and watch the 95% CI shift with it. Notice: the CI either captures the true mean or it doesn't. SE = 0.14, so ME = 1.96 × 0.14 = ±0.274.
